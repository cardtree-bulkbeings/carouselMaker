# Three worlds, one universe

v1 (tag `v1-reference-skins`) gave each handle a *style*. This gives each handle a *world*: a place
with its own beliefs, objects, vocabulary and visual grammar. Every carousel is an **artifact from
that world**. A folio from a codex, a memo from a bureau, a quest card from a game.

The starting idea (Founder = Dune × Renaissance, Company = Apple × 2001, Agent Bro = cyberpunk) is
right in spirit. Tested against our research, the content plan and the assets we own, each one needs
a turn.

---

## The guardrail: world in the grammar, plain words in the headline

Worlds fail on Instagram when the world gets in the way of the 1-second decision. So:

| World lives in… | Never in… |
|---|---|
| labels, kickers, badges, numbering ("Folio III", "Dept. of Voice", "Quest 2/5") | the headline. Headlines stay plain, concrete, 3–8 words |
| artifacts (law cards, ID badges, loot cards), frames, textures, seals | the readability. Light grounds, high contrast, big type |
| the CTA's flavour ("Comment CODEX", "Unlock the workflow") | the promise. The cover still says what you get |

And every world keeps the research rules: a face with direct gaze or a character on the cover,
one idea per slide, a standalone slide 2, a light ask at the end. All 21 references that performed
were **light-ground**. No world goes dark by default; a dark slide is a once-per-post beat.

## 1. Founder → The Codex (the Architect)

**Not Dune alone. Leonardo's notebooks, with Dune's restraint.**

Why the turn: the plan's founder posts are mostly *builds* ("How I built an AI receptionist",
"I built an AI sales team", "Meet my AI team") and *legends* ("I turned Chanakya into my advisor",
"An AI board: Jobs, Buffett, Hormozi"). Pure Dune monumentalism makes a dentist receptionist feel
pompous, and awe is a slow reward. Leonardo is the figure who both **thought and built**: philosophy,
anatomy and machines in one notebook. That's exactly a founder who builds AI agents and argues with
Chanakya. Dune lends the scale, the negative space and the gravity (councils, doctrines, houses).

- **Human truth:** "I want to understand how the world works, and build my own piece of it."
- **Emotion:** awe → mastery. *"This person has a worldview, not just content."*
- **References:** Leonardo's Codex Atlanticus (annotated machines, marginalia) · Renaissance altarpieces
  (the figure in an arched niche) · Dune 2021/24 (monumental negative space, sigils, councils) ·
  Monocle / The Gentlewoman (modern editorial serif) · meghanify's editorial serif (2.8K–6.1K likes,
  proof this audience reads serif).
- **Objects in this world:** folios · laws · annotated diagrams with leader lines · the council table
  · portrait medallions · wax seals · maps and timelines · marginal notes in ink.
- **Vocabulary (labels only):** Folio I / II · Law · Doctrine · The Council · Counsel of {person} ·
  Margin note · Sealed.
- **Visual grammar:** parchment/bone ground, iron-gall ink, **one pigment, ultramarine** (our cobalt
  is literally the Renaissance's most precious blue) and sealing-wax red only for seals. High-contrast
  editorial serif headlines, roman numerals, thin double rules, axial symmetry, wide margins.
  **The signature:** the founder stands in an **arched niche** like a figure in an altarpiece.
- **Format mapping:** F02 build = folios of a machine · F01 persona = "Counsel of Chanakya" with a
  medallion portrait · F04 team = The Council · F03 habits = Laws · F05 flow = a codex diagram.

## 2. Company → The Bureau (the Institution)

**Not Apple × 2001. Monsters, Inc. × Wes Anderson × Apple.**

Why the turn: The Double's world is already **felt plush agents and clay people in warm sets**.
2001's cold machine is the exact fear our objection posts fight ("AI sounds robotic / fake",
"will it replace me"). The research says affect and characters carry information. Monsters, Inc. is
the template: **a company populated by characters with jobs, departments, ID badges and shifts**.
That's our crew. Wes Anderson adds institutional symmetry, deadpan signage, pastel blocks and fake
paperwork (The French Dispatch is a whole magazine of artifacts). Apple keeps the product shots honest.

- **Human truth:** "I want a team that has my back, even while I sleep."
- **Emotion:** relief → delight. *"Wait, they do that for me?"*
- **References:** Monsters, Inc. (company of characters, departments, badges) · Wes Anderson,
  *Grand Budapest* / *French Dispatch* / *Asteroid City* (symmetry, signage, forms, pastels) ·
  Pixar's WALL-E / Inside Out (feelings in characters) · Animal Crossing (a friendly institution) ·
  theonlyworkshop + Wellbeing (the calm grid we already proved).
- **Objects in this world:** staff ID badges · department signs · inter-office memos · shift logs and
  time cards · case files · forms with checkboxes · org charts · "employee of the month".
- **Vocabulary (labels only):** Dept. of Knowledge / Personality / Voice / Channels / Actions (the
  brand's five capabilities become five **departments**, with their colours) · Night shift · Shift log ·
  Memo · Case file · Staff #004 · Onboarding.
- **Visual grammar:** warm cream, perfectly centred and symmetrical, Futura-like geometric caps for
  signage (Jost), calm sans headlines, department pastels as colour blocks, felt agents on every slide
  wearing their badge. No humans, ever.
- **Format mapping:** F03 = staff directory (one ID badge per agent) · F06 = night-shift log ·
  F07 = performance review · F08 = "Case file: which reply was AI?" · F01 collab = a staff profile.

## 3. Agent Bro → The Quest Board (the Player)

**Not cyberpunk. The game UI itself: quests, loot, patch notes, achievements.**

Why the turn: dark neon cyberpunk is the default skin of every AI-hype account. It isn't a purple
cow, and neon-on-black reads badly at thumbnail size. What makes games addictive isn't the setting,
it's the **interface grammar**: a quest with steps, loot with rarity, a progress bar, an achievement
pop. That turns every carousel into something to *play*, which is the research's participation hook,
and it fits his plan exactly (prompts, skills, tools, hot takes, news).

- **Human truth:** "I want to level up, and play with the new toys before everyone else."
- **Emotion:** excitement → mastery. *"Ok, I'm trying this tonight."*
- **References:** Persona 5 (red/black/white, slashed cut-out labels, highest-energy UI in games) ·
  Pokémon / Magic cards (stat blocks, types, rarity) · Zelda / Elden Ring item descriptions ·
  Duolingo & Strava (streaks, XP) · Balatro (juicy feedback) · theaiagents (the density that worked).
- **Objects in this world:** quest cards (difficulty, time, reward) · loot cards with rarity ·
  loadout slots · XP bars · patch notes · boss cards · achievement toasts · player card.
- **Vocabulary (labels only):** Quest · Objective 2/5 · Loot · Rarity (common / rare / epic /
  legendary) · Loadout · Patch notes · Boss fight · Unlocked · LVL · XP.
- **Visual grammar:** paper-white ground, black ink, **one hot red** for energy, rarity colours only on
  loot. Heavy grotesk headlines (readable), slashed skewed label blocks (Persona), mono HUD labels,
  pixel numerals for XP. An XP bar replaces the progress dots. His photo on the cover as a player card.
- **Format mapping:** F02 = Quest · F09 = Loot drop (each prompt a loot card) · F10 = Inventory ·
  F03 = Loadout · F11 = Boss fight · F12 = Patch notes · F07 = Versus screen (fighting-game select).

## One universe

| | Founder | Company | Agent Bro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role | The Architect | The Bureau | The Player |
| Answers | **Why** this changes things | **How** it works for you | **What** you can do tonight |
| Time | the long view | the working day | right now |
| Object | the codex | the badge | the quest card |
| Emotion | awe | relief | excitement |

They meet without merging. A new model launches: Agent Bro posts the patch notes, the Bureau
"hires" it into a department, the Founder writes the law it proves. A Founder build can show a
Bureau ID badge for the agent he built. A Company case file can cite a quest a player completed.
Agent Bro stays his own person: he can visit the Bureau, but never wears its badge.

## Building it

The worlds are new design languages next to v1, not replacements:

| Handle | v1 brand (kept) | World brand |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | `thedouble-founder` (bold) | `founder-codex` (codex) |
| Company | `thedouble-company` (calm) | `company-bureau` (bureau) |
| Agent Bro | `agentbro` (studio) | `agentbro-quest` (quest) |

Switch a post between them by changing `brand:`. Roll back everything: `git checkout v1-reference-skins`.

## v2 (after review): airy, and stolen from many

Feedback on v2.0: the grounds (parchment, cream) read dark and dull, the fonts were scattered, and
Agent Bro looked like an ad. All three worlds now sit on **white**, use **two type families**, and
present like the references (a carousel sheet, not a poster). Each element is borrowed from several
references, never copied from one (*Steal Like an Artist*):

| Element | Founder · Codex | Company · Bureau | Agent Bro · Quest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | white + faint pigment glow (aiagently) | pure white (theonlyworkshop, wellbeing) | white + soft accent glows (aiagently) |
| Headline | editorial serif, centred (meghanify) | tight sans, centred (theonlyworkshop) | heavy sans, left (theaiagents, algowinner) |
| Emphasis | italic in ultramarine | marker underline in mustard | one hot red word (algowinner) |
| Label | tinted pill + italic roman numeral (aiagently pill) | mono caps label (theonlyworkshop) | gradient pill + grey caps subtitle (theaiagents) |
| Cards | soft pigment tint, no borders (meghanify) | white, soft shadow; pastel badges | white, soft shadow; rarity colour on loot only |
| Progress | "Next folio" pill (aiagently) | "Next on shift →" mono footer (theonlyworkshop) | slim XP bar + "Next" pill |
| World artifact | Laws, wax seal, arched niche, medallion | ID badges, memo, rubber stamp, departments | quest chips, loot rarity, achievement chip |
| Ending | keyword pill in pigment | "Comment ‘WORD’" marker pill | huge COMMENT + keyword (aiagently) |

App UIs (WhatsApp, iMessage, Instagram, call) are now one self-contained kit (`engine/chat.css`) that
matches the real light-mode apps and is never restyled by a world.
