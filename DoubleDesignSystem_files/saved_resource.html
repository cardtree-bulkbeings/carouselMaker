The ten rules

  1. Calm ground. ground (linen) and paper carry most of every piece. Night uses the same

rules on the dark ground.

  1. One bold object per view. A full-strength block, a character, a render or a big number.

If two are visible, one goes soft.

  1. Color says what it does. Capability colors only: knowledge (grape), personality

(bubblegum), voice (sky), channels (cobalt), actions (tangerine), in that setup order. Audiences never get a color. Results (earnings, analytics) are ink.

  1. Lime means live. live is a small square or chip with a word. Never a ground, a card or a

chart series. No green anywhere.

  1. Heavy sans, soft italic. Headlines in display 700, sentence case, tight. One human

italic phrase per headline: accent on light, accent-on-deep on deep, live on cobalt.

  1. Text follows lightness. White on cobalt, grape, deep, cobalt-deep. Ink on sky,

tangerine, bubblegum, live, every soft tint and every wash. 4.5:1, 3:1 at 24px+.

  1. AI, without the gradient. The AI feel comes from pixel lenses, Silkscreen status lines, the

lime live square, Aida grids and stitch paths. No glows, neon or blue-purple gradients.

  1. Code first, AI second. Grounds, washes, textures, type and logo are rendered by us. AI

renders fill only the object slot, never carry text.

  1. Solid, soft, no outlines. Radii from radius-*, separation by fill, no card borders or

shadows. Only overlays use shadow-pop.

  1. Say it plainly. Verb-first buttons ("Build your Double"), numerals, real numbers, no hype,

no "!" in headlines, US English.

Voice

Write like a person who happens to be very capable.

  • Human, not robotic. People, time, conversations.
  • Confident, not hype. State what it does. No "revolutionary", "unleash", no "!" in headlines.
  • Specific, not vague. "Books the meeting in your calendar", not "streamlines workflows".
  • Warm, not silly. One smile per section, usually the italic phrase or a twist.

Same voice, different volume:

Headline formulas

  1. Benefit + human phrase (default): bold claim in sans, the human part in italic.
  2. The twist: two short sentences, the second flips the first. "You're offline. Your Double isn't."
  3. Beyond the obvious: correct an assumption, prove it with a list of actions.
  4. Say what it is: plain explanation, for heroes and first-time readers.
  5. Two of you: the core idea in five words. Posters, OG images, launches.
  6. Invitation: closing CTAs. A question, never a command with "!".

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for headlines, buttons, nav, titles. Uppercase only for mono labels and status.
  • One italic phrase per headline, never the whole line.
  • Statements end with a period. No serial comma ("knowledge, voice and rules").
  • Numerals always (5 minutes, 30+ languages, 24/7). Real numbers only. US English.
  • "&" only in labels, tabs and nav.
  • Buttons: verb + object ("Connect WhatsApp"). Never "Submit", "Click here".
  • Status lines: pixel type, past tense, arrow for destination ("Lead saved → Salesforce").
  • Empty state: what's missing + why it matters + one action.
  • Error: what happened + how to fix, no blame, no "Oops".
  • Success: say what changed ("Voice saved. Your Double sounds like you now.").
  • Disclosure: a Double says it's an AI Double of a named person when asked, and on its first

message by default.

Boilerplate

  • 10 words: AI Doubles that talk and act for you, across channels.
  • 30 words: The Double is a Digital Twin OS. Build an AI Double from your knowledge, voice and

rules, and it answers questions, shares your expertise and gets things done across every channel.

  • 60 words: see the brand viewer (build/brand-viewer.html) until copied here verbatim.

Capabilities

Five, in setup order: Knowledge → Personality → Voice → Channels → Actions.

  • A thing's color = its primary capability, not the page it's on and not its department. A

voice picker on the Identity page is sky, not bubblegum.

  • Max one capability color per card, plus up to three small squares for secondary capabilities.
  • Cobalt does double duty on purpose: brand and Channels. In product, brand moments use deep or ink.
  • Results are neutral: Earnings, Analytics, Inbox, Contacts, Settings, Home use ink.

State

  • Lime = live. On, live, connected, saved, new, completed. Chip-sized, never a background.

The only lime in forms is a switch that's on. Only the live square pulses.

  • Amber needs attention (including "limit reached"). Red failed or destructive. Grey

paused, draft, off.

  • A full plan (20/20 Doubles) is warn, not error.
  • Money going out in a ledger is ink with a minus sign, not red.
  • No green anywhere. "Connected" is live (lime square + word).

Budget

  • Linen ground and white paper: the calm, most of every screen.
  • Soft capability tints: on items of that capability (tags, tiles, cards in marketing).
  • Deep: once per page.
  • Full-strength color: objects only (hero block, character, icon tile, CTA). **Bold is an object,

never a wall:** rounded, with margin around it.

  • One full block per screen. If two are visible, one goes soft.
  • Every soft card gets one full-strength thing (character, icon tile, status square).

Surfaces

  • Product is calmer than marketing. No tinted page bands, no textures, no full-color blocks

behind content. Color only for capability, state and the Double.

  • Web and content may use capability bands, felt, textures and deep sections, within budget.

Text on color

Text follows lightness. Cobalt, grape, deep, cobalt-deep take white. Sky, tangerine, bubblegum, lime take ink. Soft tints always take ink (light) or ink (dark theme values). Minimum 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 at 24px+. States never rely on color alone.

Charts

  • Series follow capability order: grape, bubblegum, sky, cobalt, tangerine.
  • When series aren't capabilities: highlight one series in the page's capability color, the rest

ink-3 / fill-3.

  • State colors are never series. Direct labels over legends. Gridlines in line.

Palette limits (decided 2026-10-02)

  • No yellow hue, no seventh color. Lime (live) and amber (warn) already sit in the yellow

range; a yellow brand hue would blur both. In content only, amber may color a prop (a star, a sticker, a coin) or underline one word in a thumbnail. It never colors a ground, a card or text.

  • No lavender. The old lavender palette is retired. The only light violet is knowledge-soft,

and it appears only on knowledge things.

  • Renders made before the refresh (lavender sets, older people) are re-rendered, not re-tinted.

Day and Night

Night swaps neutrals, soft tints, deep and accent (see tokens.json). Full-strength capability and state colors stay the same. Primary button becomes a light pill with on-ink text.

08-cast.md

Cast: people and agents

Status: rules agreed, designs next. Character design is the next phase; until it lands, use only the existing renders in Assets › Characters and Scenes, and never invent a new face.

What is decided

  • A finite, fixed cast. We are known for a few characters and reuse them everywhere. Each has a

name, a fixed look and a sheet. No one-off people.

  • Core + supporting, for humans and for agents. Proposed: 6 core humans + 4 supporting;

5 core agents (one per capability) + supporting agents.

  • Diverse by design: gender, age, skin tone, hair, body type and, where possible, nationality.

Spread across the core set, not saved for the supporting one.

  • Humans: felt and clay, warm and tactile, pixel-lens glasses (the brand's signature). Clothes

in the new palette only (linen, ink, cobalt, deep, capability colors). No lavender.

  • Agents: felt bodies, a screen face with pixel eyes, a lime antenna, one capability color each.

Because the Double talks, agents get a voice face (mouth or speaking signal), plus hair and accessories that give them personality and presentation.

  • One body language for both: the agents' eyes and the humans' glasses share the pixel lens, so

a person and their Double read as a pair.

  • Existing human renders are re-rendered in the new palette before reuse.

Open (decide with mocks)

  1. Voice face: pixel audio-bar mouth vs a simple drawn mouth.
  2. Eye pixels: exact logo lens vs a finer, rounder "stitched" pixel for more expression.
  3. Silhouettes: one body for all agents (accessories differ) vs one silhouette per agent.
  4. Names and roles for the core cast.

Placement rules (hold now)

  • Humans in story scenes (a person and their Double); agents in product and "it's working"

moments. Never two heroes in one piece.

  • A character sits on the empty-wall side opposite the words, on a white tile or a soft stage.
  • Characters never hold text, logos or UI with readable words.

09-product.md

Surface: product (the app)

The app is the calm surface. Its rules and tokens live in cardtree-client (docs/design-system/README.md, tokens.md, components in src/components/ui). Read those for any UI work; this page is only the summary.

  • Calm at rest, color on touch. Ink, ground and paper carry every screen. A capability color

appears on hover, select and its icon tile.

  • No washes, textures, felt, tinted page bands or full-color blocks behind content.
  • One primary button per view (ink). Cobalt buttons are for upgrade and plan actions only.
  • Type: the nine ui-* steps in tokens.json. No italic except onboarding and sign-in.
  • Separation by fill, not borders; only overlays cast shadow-pop.
  • Status: StatusLabel (Silkscreen + square). Lime = live.
  • Both themes and 375px wide, always.
